Good to Know

Getting Around

Get an ORCA card or use the Transit GO Ticket app the moment you land — it covers Link light rail and the monorail. Flat $3 fare per light rail ride.

Weather

Early August in Seattle is usually mild and dry (mid-60s–70s°F), but bring a light layer for evenings and the ballpark — it cools off after sunset.

Ballpark Bag Policy

T-Mobile Park uses a clear-bag policy. Small clutch/wristlet bags without the clear requirement are also allowed — check current size limits before you go.

Cruise Prep

Complete Holland America's online check-in and print or download boarding documents before Saturday. Keep passport, medications, and anything you need same-day in a carry-on — checked luggage may not reach your cabin for a few hours.

Hotel Timing

Standard check-in at the Westin is usually 3 PM and checkout 12 PM — both work naturally around this schedule, but confirm at booking since early check-in on Friday morning isn't guaranteed.

Pier Confirmation

Holland America sails from Pier 91 (Smith Cove) — double-check this on your cruise documents, since Pier 66 downtown serves other cruise lines and is a different location.
