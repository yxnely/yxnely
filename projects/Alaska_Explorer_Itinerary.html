Seattle, Washington

Saturday, August 8 · Embarkation · departs 3:00pm
78° / 59°F Mild & dry

No activities booked — an easy boarding day. Aim to be at the terminal by early afternoon, then head straight to the top deck for sail-away as you pass under the Seattle skyline into Puget Sound.

🍽 Eat
  • Pike Place Market, before boarding~11:00am – 12:30pmGrab a grilled-salmon salad or poke bowl rather than the fried classics — light enough to still enjoy the sail-away toast.
  • Main Dining Room, onboardOpen seating 5:15 – 7:00pmKeep night one simple after a travel day: grilled fish or chicken entrée with the vegetable side.
  • Pike Place Chowder~11:30am, in a sourdough bread bowlThe line moves fast and it's a Seattle classic worth the calories.
⛴ Onboard
  • Sail-away on the Lido DeckLive music and a view of the skyline as you pull out of Puget Sound.

2SUN

At Sea

Sunday, August 9 · Cruising toward the Inside Passage
64° / 55°F Breezy on deck

A full sea day — the water cools noticeably as you head north. Good day to lay out tomorrow's layers and charge every battery before Juneau. If Holland America has an onboard naturalist lecture scheduled, past travelers say it meaningfully improves the wildlife-spotting days ahead.

🍽 Eat
  • Lido Market, breakfast7:00 – 10:00amBuild a protein plate — eggs, smoked salmon, fruit — and skip the pastry case.
  • Lido Market or Dive-In, lunch11:30am – 1:30pmSalad bar plus grilled chicken or fish instead of the burger stand.
  • Tamarind, dinner~6:00pm, before the evening showPan-Asian small plates make it easy to control portions — share a few rather than over-ordering.
  • Dive-In cheeseburger & friesPoolside, any time it's openClassic cruise poolside indulgence — best enjoyed in a deck chair.
⛴ Onboard
  • Culinary Arts Center demoA hands-on cooking class or chef demo — check the daily program for timing.
  • Greenhouse Spa thermal suiteA quiet, warm reset before the busier port days start.
  • Trivia in the Ocean BarA relaxed way to spend the late afternoon before dinner.
🧭 Do & shop
  • Gym or Promenade Deck walkA lap of the Promenade Deck is roughly a third of a mile — good for a movement break between meals.
3MON

Juneau, Alaska

Monday, August 10 · In port 12:30pm – 10:00pm
58° / 48°F 58% chance of rain
Booked · Both travelers
Mendenhall Glacier Helicopter & Guided Walk
3:45pm departure
Insider tip — if you have any free time before or after the flight, the Nugget Falls Photo Point Trail is a short, flat walk directly across the lake from Mendenhall Glacier and worth the detour. Bring binoculars — mountain goats are regularly spotted on the ridgeline above the visitor center.
🍽 Eat
  • Alaska Fish & Chips Co., lunch~12:45 – 1:30pm, before your 3:45pm tourOrder the fish tacos or a salmon salad instead of the fried basket to stay light before the helicopter.
  • Tracy's King Crab Shack, early dinner~6:00 – 7:00pm, after the tour landsStraightforward steamed crab — split a portion between you, no heavy sauce needed.
  • Tracy's whole king crab legSame visit, go all in this timeExtra garlic butter, no splitting — it's Juneau's most famous plate for a reason.
⛴ Onboard
  • Evening show or casinoOnce you're back around 7pm, check the World Stage program before all-aboard.
🧭 Do & shop
  • South Franklin StreetNative art galleries and the Alaskan Brewing Co. tasting room, steps from the dock.
  • Mount Roberts TramwayIf time allows after the tour, a quick ride up for a Gastineau Channel view.
4TUE

Glacier Bay & Icy Strait Point

Tuesday, August 11 · Scenic cruising 7:00am – 4:00pm · in port 6:00pm – 10:00pm
57° / 47°F Cold near ice
Booked · Both travelers
The ZipRider Adventure & Sky Peak Gondola
7:30pm departure, Icy Strait Point
From past sailings — sunrise is around 6am and the ship often enters Glacier Bay right around then, so get to sleep the night before. Go out on deck rather than watching from the cabin — you'll hear the glacier calve, which you can't from inside. Port-side decks tend to get the closer glacier views on this route. Layer up, bring sunblock (the glare off the ice is real), and keep binoculars around your neck — otters and whales don't linger.
🍽 Eat
  • Coffee & fruit on your balcony~6:00 – 6:30amA small bite so you don't have to choose between breakfast and sunrise glacier viewing.
  • Lido Market, full breakfast8:00 – 9:30am, once past the closest viewing
  • Duck Point Smokehouse or Crab House, Icy Strait Point~8:30 – 9:30pm, after the ZipRiderSmoked salmon or crab — both naturally protein-forward with little added prep.
  • Duck Point Surf & Turf with crab tater totsSame stop, same windowA locals' favorite, and hard to justify skipping after a ZipRider adrenaline hit.
⛴ Onboard
  • Crow's Nest observation loungeThe best vantage point for glacier viewing, with a ranger narrating over the PA during the cruise-through.
  • Deck barbecueHolland America often runs an outdoor lunch grill on Glacier Bay day — worth checking the program.
🧭 Do & shop
  • The Cannery shopsAll Alaskan-owned — jewelry, art and canned Sockeye salmon to bring home.
  • Icy Strait BrewingA short stroll from the pier if you have time before or after the ZipRider.
